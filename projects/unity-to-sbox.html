Moving a Unity scene to s&box

I built a scene and asset conversion pipeline to bring an existing Unity environment into s&box without rebuilding its object placements and material assignments by hand.

SCENE IMPORT PIPELINE

Capture the scene separately from its assets

I exported scene objects to JSON with their position, forward and up vectors, scale, parent path, and a flag for mirrored objects. Separate conversion tools turned FBX meshes into VMDL assets and PNG textures into VMAT materials. That let me debug asset conversion separately from object placement.

Translate transforms between engines

The importer remapped the position axes, applied a unit conversion, and rebuilt rotations from forward and up vectors. Mirrored objects were flagged during export and baked in Blender where needed. That gave negative scale an explicit path through the pipeline.

Validate against a real scene

I used a casino and town environment to check the import. Most of the scene came across, although a few glass panels and other props still needed placement fixes. Unity’s per-asset import scale and offsets are the next settings I’d check against those remaining discrepancies.
