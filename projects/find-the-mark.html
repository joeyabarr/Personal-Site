Find the Mark

Turning clues into a game jam prototype

Find the Mark is a Unity game jam prototype about identifying a target from clues in a crowd. The player has limited time and ammo and needs enough confidence to act.

  • Prototype
  • Unity
  • C#
  • Cinemachine
CLUES
GAME JAM PROTOTYPE

Make the clue system drive the round

The jam theme was Hide and Seek. I turned it into a deduction problem where the target blends into the crowd. Clues give the player a reason to observe the other characters before acting, and limited time and ammo make each decision count.

Build the interaction first

I worked on a mouse-follow camera with Cinemachine, integrated character animations, and built a start menu with an animated character. Those pieces gave me a way to test the feel of observing and aiming before building out the crowd behavior.

What I’d test next

The next pieces are NPC wandering, upper-body aiming, and the timer and retry flow. With a repeatable round in place, I’d test whether the clues are legible under pressure and whether movement in the crowd makes them more interesting to use.
