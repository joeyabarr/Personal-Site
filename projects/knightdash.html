Building a campus delivery drone

I led a student team developing an autonomous delivery drone for campus. The project brought together software, hardware, controls, and integration, and placed first at UCF’s Engineering Week Project Showcase.

  • Project Lead
  • UCF
  • 2020–2023
AUTONOMOUS DELIVERY

The challenge

Campus delivery was the use case. Building an autonomous aircraft meant working across teams whose pieces had to function together. A change to the physical design or controls could affect what the software needed to do.

My role

I led the project across software, hardware, controls, and integration teams. My responsibility was to keep the overall delivery goal in view while each group worked on a different part of the aircraft.

The outcome

We took first place at UCF’s Engineering Week Project Showcase. The result I’m proudest of is bringing several engineering disciplines together around a single project.
